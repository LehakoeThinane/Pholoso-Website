question: Are donations tax-deductible?
---

We are a Public Benefit Organisation approved by SARS under Section 18A (PBO 930090077). If you pay tax in South Africa, your donation may be tax-deductible when you have a valid Section 18A receipt, within the limits SARS sets. Please check with SARS or a tax adviser.
