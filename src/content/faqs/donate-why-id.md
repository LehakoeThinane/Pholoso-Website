question: Why do you ask for my ID number and address?
---

Since 1 March 2023, SARS requires more details on Section 18A receipts, including the donor's identity or registration number and address. We must also report every receipt we issue to SARS as third-party data (IT3(d)). We use these details only to issue your receipt and report it to SARS.
