# Keyword map: homepage only (Part A)

This first version covers the homepage only, because Part A keeps the single-page site. It will be expanded to one primary intent per page in the rebuild (Part B), with research per language.

The queries below are **reasoned starting points, not measured search volumes**. Check them in Google Search Console once the site is verified (see DEPLOY.md), and in a keyword tool if one is available.

## Homepage: `https://pholoso.africa/`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Primary intent** | Find a South African organisation working on education for neurodivergent children, and decide whether to trust, contact, partner with or fund it. |
| **Audiences** | Parents and caregivers; educators and schools; funders, CSI and ESG teams; partners. |
| **Primary query** | neurodivergent learners South Africa |
| **Secondary queries** | inclusive education South Africa · neurodiversity organisation South Africa · support for neurodivergent children South Africa · autism education NPO South Africa · Pholoso Afrika Foundation · 2to.iQ |
| **Question-style queries** | where can I get support for my neurodivergent child in South Africa? · which NPOs support inclusive education in South Africa? · are donations to education NPOs tax-deductible in South Africa (Section 18A)? |
| **Main call to action** | Get involved (partner, donate, schools) |
| **Secondary call to action** | Contact |

## What changed on the homepage

| Item | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `<title>` | "Pholoso Afrika Foundation \| Inclusive, neurodiversity-first education in South Africa" (85 characters, cut off in results) | "Neurodivergent learners · Pholoso Afrika Foundation" (51) |
| Meta description | 218 characters, cut off in results | "Pholoso Afrika Foundation advances inclusive, adaptive education for neurodivergent children in South Africa. Partner with us, donate or get in touch." (150) |
| Title after navigating | The script replaced it with "Home \| Pholoso Afrika Foundation" | The homepage keeps its title; inner sections use "Section · Pholoso Afrika Foundation" |

"Neurodiversity-first" stays in the page copy as the Foundation's own phrase, but people rarely search for it, so it is no longer the lead term in the title.

## Gaps to close in Part B

- The single-page site has one URL, so the About, Stories and Resources sections cannot rank on their own. Part B gives each its own URL and title.
- Local, practical queries (for example "autism assessment Johannesburg", "Care Dependency Grant autism", "SIAS policy explained") need real Resource Centre and pathway pages. They cannot be targeted honestly from the current site.
- Research per language for each published locale.
